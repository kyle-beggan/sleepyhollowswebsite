Join Us for the Annual Sleepy Hollows Invitational

Dust off your clubs for a day of fairways, greens, and good company. The Sleepy Hollows Invitational brings together our local community, musicians, and sponsors for a premier golf outing. Registration includes greens fees, cart, range balls, box lunch, and non-alcoholic beverages. A significant portion of each registration fee is donated directly to Herndon Regional Youth Ensembles to support youth music programs in Northern Virginia.
