Our Story

Sleepy Hollows Studio is a premium recording and production facility nestled in Historic Herndon, VA.

What started as a passionate bedroom recording setup by founder Neal "Bones" Beggan has grown over the years into a state-of-the-art multi-room production complex. Our facility is meticulously designed to facilitate creativity, collaboration, and high-fidelity sonic capture.

Whether you are a solo songwriter tracking your first acoustic demo or a full band tracking a heavy rock album, we provide the environment, resources, and experienced engineering to bring your vision to life.

"We operate on the principle that the studio should feel like a second home. Creativity thrives when artists feel comfortable, supported, and inspired."
